How to record your phone screen
  1. Swipe down for Control Centre. No ⏺ button? Add it in Control Centre using its + button.
  2. Long-press the ⏺ button and make sure Microphone is off — you want the video's own sound, not the room.
  3. Tap Start Recording, wait for the countdown, then play your video.
  4. Tap the Screen Recording indicator → Stop. iOS saves it to Photos.
  5. Come back here and tap Import a recording.

Some apps block audio or video recording. Check that your saved clip has sound before importing.
